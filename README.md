# Baba Is You Takeover

**Terraria** × Baba Is You · visual · idea by the team

Bring Baba Is You into the game in the most visible and funny way. The change shows in the first 30 seconds of play.

> I turned Terraria into Baba Is You: floating word blocks rewrite the rules, and my zombies turned into little white Babas.

## Install

Copy Sigf.tmod into Documents/My Games/Terraria/tModLoader/Mods and enable it in tModLoader's Manage Mods menu. Load any world: you become a Baba, and word blocks float above spawn. Hit them with arrows or swords to rewrite the rules.
